A Very Special Supporter Joined Us on Our Walk! 💙🚶‍♂️

We were delighted to welcome the son of one of our members on our recent walk on Thursday 10th September at Chorlton Water Park, who came along to show his support for our group.

He is Darren Campbell MBE, former British sprint athlete, Olympic medallist and international champion. Darren has represented his country at the highest level and has been recognised with an MBE for his achievements and contribution to sport.

It was a real pleasure to have him join us, share some time with the group and support what we do. Having someone of Darren’s experience and achievements walking alongside us made the day even more special.

A huge thank you, Darren, for coming along and supporting us. You’re welcome to join us anytime! 💙

Together we walk, together we support, together we make a difference. 👣
