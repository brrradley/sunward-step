Join us Learn more

A safe and welcoming space

Whether you’re looking to move more, meet new people, or just enjoy the rhythm of a regular walk, Sunward Step is here for you. We’re a friendly, supportive group with a relaxed pace, a good atmosphere and plenty of room to feel comfortable at your own speed.

Our community values

We believe that being around other people can make a real difference. Sunward Step creates space for meaningful connection, encouragement and a sense of belonging for anyone who wants to enjoy walking in good company.

  • ✓Warm, welcoming and non-judgemental.
  • ✓Gentle, realistic pace for all levels and abilities.
  • ✓Regular opportunities to build friendships and confidence.
